Malaysian Culture

Culture In Malaysia

Local Delicacies

Nasi Lemak

Fragrant rice cooked in coconut milk and pandan leaf.

Satay

Seasoned, skewered and grilled meat, served with peanut sauce.

Laksa

Spicy noodle soup with a rich curry coconut milk base.

Traditional Attire

Malay: Baju Kurung

Elegant, loose-fitting traditional attire comprising a blouse and a long skirt.

Chinese: Cheongsam

A stylish, one-piece dress with a high collar and distinctive button design.

Indian: Saree

A beautiful drape of fabric, typically 6 yards long, wrapped around the waist and shoulder.

Major Festivals

Hari Raya Aidilfitri

Celebrated by Muslims to mark the end of the fasting month of Ramadan.

Chinese New Year

A grand celebration welcoming the new lunar year with lion dances and reunion dinners.

Deepavali

The 'Festival of Lights' celebrated by Hindus, symbolizing the victory of light over darkness.
